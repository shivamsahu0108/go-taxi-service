How to choose the right rental vehicle

Start with the number of passengers and luggage, then consider the length of the journey and the number of stops. A sedan may suit a short airport transfer or small family. An Ertiga or Innova Crysta can provide more space for longer travel. Groups travelling for a wedding, corporate event or tour may need a Tempo Traveller, Urbania, mini bus or larger coach.

For event transport, share the movement schedule, pickup points, ceremony timing and return plan. For a local rental, share the approximate hours and areas to be covered. Go Taxi can then check the appropriate vehicle and explain the fare, driver hours, tolls, parking and outstation terms.
