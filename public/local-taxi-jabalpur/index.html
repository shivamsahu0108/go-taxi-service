How local taxi fares are confirmed

The fare depends on the vehicle, distance, number of stops, waiting time and whether the booking is point-to-point or hourly. For sightseeing, share the places you want to visit and the expected duration. For a simple transfer, send both pickup and drop locations.

Go Taxi confirms practical trip details directly by phone or WhatsApp. This lets the team account for traffic, luggage and the actual vehicle size instead of showing one generic price for every customer.
