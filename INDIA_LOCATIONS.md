# India City Location Autocomplete

The booking form now uses `src/data/indiaLocations.js` for pickup and drop autocomplete.

Each option is standardized as `City, State/UT`, so the selected location remains clear in the WhatsApp booking message.

The list includes major and commonly searched cities/towns across all Indian states and Union Territories, plus quick Jabalpur pickup points such as Jabalpur Railway Station and Dumna Airport.
